BBC Learning English — Test Your Level

Source: bbc.co.uk/learningenglish · 3 tests · 15 questions each

Test 1 (Session 1)

Q1. Which of these sentences containing adverbs is correct?
a)I worked hardly.
b)He ran quickly.
c)I arrived lately for the meeting.
Q2. Which present perfect sentence is correct?
a)We lived in the UK since 2010.
b)We’ve lived in the UK for 10 years.
c)We’ve lived in the UK since 10 years.
Q3. Which sentence means the speaker finds the temperature uncomfortable?
a)It's too hot today.
b)It's very hot today.
c)It's really hot today.
Q4. Complete this sentence: I love the autumn because…
a)…the trees are too beautiful!
b)…the trees are such beautiful!
c)…the trees are so beautiful!
Q5. Which of these sentences is correct?
a)Do we have coffee enough?
b)Some students don’t sleep enough.
c)He’s not tall enough reach the top of the cupboard.
Q6. Which word best fits in the space if you want to make a strong recommendation? We\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ visit Aunt Mary soon. We haven’t seen her in years!
a)could
b)must
c)shouldn't
Q7. Choose the correct form of the verb ‘have’ to complete the sentence: If I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ enough money, I would buy a new car!
a)have
b)would have
c)had
Q8. Which sentence means that something isn't necessary?
a)You don’t have to get a taxi – the underground is really fast.
b)You haven’t to get a taxi – the underground is really fast.
c)You mustn’t get a taxi – the underground is really fast.
Q9. Which sentence uses a preposition of place correctly?
a)I saw Helen on the supermarket today.
b)I saw Helen in the supermarket today.
c)I saw Helen with the supermarket today.
Q10. Which sentence uses ‘at’ correctly?
a)We’re leaving at 3 o’clock.
b)Let’s meet at tomorrow.
c)My birthday’s at July.
Q11. In which sentence does the connecting word make sense?
a)We arrived late at the cinema because we missed the beginning of the film.
b)Because we arrived late at the cinema, we missed the beginning of the film.
c)We arrived late at the cinema but we missed the beginning of the film.
Q12. Choose the correct tag to complete this question: She’s got beautiful eyes, \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_?
a)…aren’t they?
b)…does she?
c)...hasn’t she?
Q13. Choose the correct words to complete the sentence: I went to Oxford Street \_\_\_\_\_\_ some new clothes.
a)for buy
b)to buy
c)for buying
Q14. Choose the correct word to complete the sentence: The victim was \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to hospital in an ambulance.
a)taking
b)took
c)taken
Q15. Change this sentence using reported speech: “There was an accident by the traffic lights this morning.”
a)She said there has been an accident by the traffic lights this morning.
b)She said there had been an accident by the traffic lights in the morning.
c)She said there had been an accident by the traffic lights that morning.

Test 2 (Session 2)

Q1. Which sentence uses the present continuous correctly?
a)“I think you’re telling me lies. I’m not believing you!”
b)“I’m not checking my email. I’m studying!”
c)“Please explain that to me again. I’m not understanding you.”
Q2. “In 2017, I started studying Spanish. In 2019, I visited Spain for the first time.” Which sentence correctly describes these events?
a)I have been studying Spanish for two years before I first visited Spain.
b)Before I first visited Spain, I have been studying Spanish for two years.
c)I had been studying Spanish for two years before I first visited Spain.
Q3. Which prefix goes before 'tox' and 'crease' to create words which mean 'to remove harmful toxins' and 'to reduce'?
a)de-
b)un-
c)dis-
Q4. Which sentence uses ‘neither’ correctly?
a)Neither wallet are mine.
b)Neither of these wallets aren’t mine.
c)Neither of these wallets is mine.
Q5. Which phrasal verb best fits the space in this dialogue: A: When did you \_\_\_\_\_\_ up with your boyfriend? B: Oh, I told him last night. I didn’t think he would \_\_\_\_\_\_ down in tears when I told him.
a)break
b)keep
c)hurry
Q6. The following questions were asked at a tourist information office. Which question is correct?
a)Please could you give me an advice?
b)Can you give me informations about hotels?
c)Where can I find some accommodation?
Q7. In this spoken sentence, what does I‘d...I‘d represent? “If I’d seen the thief, I’d have called the police.”
a)I would + I had
b)I would + I would
c)I had + I would
Q8. ALL these sentences contain the abstract uncountable noun ‘happiness’, but only ONE is correct. Which?
a)Money can’t buy you the happiness.
b)Money can’t buy you happiness.
c)Money can’t buy you a happiness.
Q9. Which sentence is correct?
a)Lionel Messi is a world famous football player.
b)Lionel Messi is a world-famous football player.
c)Lionel Messi is a world-famous football-player.
Q10. Your work colleague is talking on the phone, explaining why he was late this morning. Which phrase would you expect to hear?
a)My train was cancelled. That’s why I’m late.
b)My train was cancelled. Thus, I’m late.
c)My train was cancelled. Therefore, I’m late.
Q11. In which sentence is the adverb placed correctly?
a)I was happy always at school.
b)I was always happy at school.
c)I was happy at school always.
Q12. Which reporting verb best fits the space? John\_\_\_\_\_\_ breaking the expensive porcelain vase.
a)insisted on
b)suggested
c)apologised for
Q13. Which sentence about a timetabled event is correct?
a)The train will depart at 07.30 every weekday.
b)The train departs at 07.30 every weekday.
c)The train is going to depart at 07.30 every weekday.
Q14. Which sentence is correct in natural spoken English?
a)“It’s first time I’ve been to America.”
b)“It’s my first time I’ve been to America.”
c)“It’s the first time I’ve been to America.”
Q15. Which sentence is correct?
a)“I used to cycle everywhere before I passed my driving test.”
b)“I’m used to cycle everywhere before I passed my driving test.”
c)“I got used to cycle everywhere before I passed my driving test.”

Test 3 (Session 3)

Q1. Which sentence uses the present perfect tense correctly?
a)I have been to Japan last year.
b)I have lost my car keys!
c)We have got married in 2016.
Q2. Which sentence uses the articles ‘a/an/the’ correctly?
a)I saw a fox last night. The fox was looking in the rubbish for food.
b)You’re a best dad ever!
c)The sugar is bad for your teeth.
Q3. Which third conditional sentence is correct?
a)If you had asked me to marry you, I’d have said ‘yes’.
b)If you would have asked me to marry you, I’d have said ‘yes’.
c)I’d have said ‘yes’ if you would have asked me to marry you.
Q4. ‘Let’ and ‘allow’ have similar meanings, but which sentence is correct?
a)Her school does not let her to wear trousers.
b)Her school does not allow her wear trousers.
c)Her school does not allow her to wear trousers.
Q5. Which sentence is correct?
a)My brother said me that he was enjoying his new job.
b)My brother said that he was enjoying his new job.
c)My brother told that he was enjoying his new job.
Q6. Which sentence using ‘unless’ is correct?
a)What will you do unless you pass your exams?
b)Come to the pub after work, unless you’re too tired.
c)You wouldn’t have crashed your car unless you were driving carefully.
Q7. Which sentence is correct?
a)This kitchen needs to cleaned.
b)This kitchen needs cleaning.
c)This kitchen needs cleaned.
Q8. Jim’s car is broken. Choose the correct adjective to complete this sentence: Jim’s old car is…
a)…knackered.
b)…tired.
c)…exhausted.
Q9. Which verb can fill ALL the gaps to make THREE correct sentences? 1. Let’s \_\_\_\_\_\_ away these old clothes to charity. 2. I’m trying to \_\_\_\_\_\_ up smoking. It’s such a bad habit! 3. You have to \_\_\_\_\_\_ way to oncoming traffic at this junction.
a)make
b)give
c)take
Q10. Which sentence using ‘wish’ is correct?
a)I wish I have studied for my exam.
b)I wish I have had studied for my exam.
c)I wish I had studied for my exam.
Q11. Choose the correct expression to complete this sentence: Reports of hurricane winds and heavy rain are increasing. Many cities have issued \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
a)a red alert
b)a blue alert
c)a green alert
Q12. Look at the infinitive – past simple – past participle forms of the verb ‘break’: break – broke – broken. Which of the following verbs has the same rhyming pattern as ‘break’?
a)speak
b)shut
c)make
Q13. Which expression can be used instead of ‘let’s go’, when you’re suggesting to leave somewhere together with others?
a)I'm off.
b)Time for me to hit the road.
c)Time to make a move.
Q14. Which sentence best uses narrative tenses to tell this story in the past?
a)I was relaxing on the beach when suddenly I was seeing storm clouds coming.
b)I relaxed on the beach when suddenly I’d seen storm clouds coming.
c)I was relaxing on the beach when suddenly I saw storm clouds coming.
Q15. According to the rules of reported speech, which sentence correctly reports this question? “When is your birthday?”
a)He asked me when my birthday is.
b)He asked me when my birthday was.
c)He asked me when was your birthday.
